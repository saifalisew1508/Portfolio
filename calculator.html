06 / LEARN

Mass concentration ↔ molar concentration

Convert mass concentration to g/L, divide by molecular weight (g/mol) to obtain mol/L, then scale to mmol/L, µmol/L or pmol/L. Reverse the steps for the opposite direction.

Enzyme activity

1 U/L = 1,000 mU/L. 1 µkat/L = 60 U/L. Enzyme activity units are not mass or molar concentration units.

Temperature

Temperature conversion uses offsets as well as scale factors. Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin cannot be converted using a simple ratio.

Reference values

Always verify the analyte, specimen, method, unit and molecular weight. This calculator is a conversion aid, not a diagnostic interpretation or replacement for your laboratory's validated procedure.
